# Affiliate Program

Partner with Stable Mesh and earn fee rebates on every transaction your referrals make.

---

## How It Works

The Stable Mesh Affiliate Program is designed for individuals and businesses who can bring volume to the platform — influencers, communities, crypto projects, agencies, and power users.

When someone signs up through your affiliate link and uses Stable Mesh, **you earn a rebate on the fees they generate**. Rebate rates are agreed individually based on your audience and expected volume.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Rebate rates are not published.** To learn the rate that applies to you, reach out via Telegram or email — see [How to Get Started](#how-to-get-started) below.
{% endhint %}

---

## Who Should Apply

- Crypto content creators and influencers
- Telegram and Discord community admins
- Freelancers and agencies managing client payments
- Businesses with high card spend volume
- Anyone with an active crypto audience

The Affiliate Program is managed directly — no automated sign-up form. Reach out to the founder to discuss your audience, expected volume, and get a custom affiliate link set up.

## FAQ

**When are rebates paid out?**
Rebate schedules are agreed upon at onboarding. Typically settled monthly in USDT or USDC.

**Is there a minimum referral volume to earn?**
No minimum — you start earning from the first referred transaction.

**Can I stack the Affiliate Program with the Invite & Earn referral program?**
Affiliate partners are set up on a custom arrangement. Discuss this with the founder when you apply.
